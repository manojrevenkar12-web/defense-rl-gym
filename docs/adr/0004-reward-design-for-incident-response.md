# 4. Pay for what a defender wanted, and pay it when it is earned

The first reward one would write for incident response, "the data did not leave", is maximised
by isolating every machine. The robust reward keeps containment and adds what the business pays
for: each innocent workstation cut off, more for a server, most for the domain controller, a
charge per tick, and credit for the share of the attacker's machines isolated.

Paid only at the end, the robust reward taught PPO from scratch to submit at once: every early
mistake costs more than giving up. Paying each decided term at the step that caused it gives the
same undiscounted return (`reward.running`, tested) and did not change that. What did was the
start: cloning the tuned rule-based analyst first, as RL on language models starts from a
supervised model. Both scratch variants are kept in the study so the failure stays visible.
